Delete your Early Bird account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Early Bird and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm with a code we send to your phone or email (or your password).

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out on every device.

Without the app

Email support@eatearlybird.com from the email address on your account, or include the phone number on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm it's you with a code sent to that phone or email, then delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • your profile: name, phone number, email, birthday, password, and the city and areas you chose;
  • favourites, saved dishes and places you've tried;
  • your consents, including every restaurant's access to your contact details (restaurants lose access straight away);
  • your reviews and review photos;
  • your Early Bird credit, available and pending, your Venuu cash, and any free booking fee pass. They can't be refunded or moved, so use them first;
  • upcoming bookings are cancelled. Cancel them yourself first if you want credit back.

What we keep, and why

  • Records of bookings and payments (amounts, dates, restaurant, Stripe reference), without your name or contact details, for 7 years, because tax and accounting law requires it. Stripe keeps its own payment records.
  • Records needed to stop fraud or resolve an open dispute or chargeback, until it's resolved.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

After deletion, your phone number and email are free to use for a new account, which starts from scratch.

You can't delete yet if

  • you're the only owner of a restaurant organisation on Early Bird: add another owner or close the organisation first;
  • a payment for a booking is still being processed: try again in a few minutes.

Questions: support@eatearlybird.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
