Use Early Bird in ChatGPT and Claude

Ask your AI assistant for a table and it finds Early Bird restaurants with 50% off food, checks open times, and books for you.

Connector address
https://mcp.eatearlybird.com

On the webChatGPT

  1. On chatgpt.com, open Plugins, tap + and choose Add custom MCP server.
  2. Name: Early Bird. Server URL: the address above. Authentication: OAuth. Tick I understand and want to continue, then Create.
  3. Early Bird asks you to sign in (email and password, or a code by email or text) and tap Allow.
  4. In a new chat, type @ and pick Early Bird, then ask:“Find me 50% off dinner in Venice for 2 on Friday.”

Custom servers work on the ChatGPT website, on plans that allow them. If you don't see Add custom MCP server, your plan or workspace doesn't offer it yet.

Web, desktop and phoneClaude

  1. Open Customize › Connectors, tap + Add and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name: Early Bird. URL: the address above. Choose Continue. Under sign-in, pick Sign in when needed (Claude may suggest No sign-in: change it, or Claude can search but not book for you), then Add.
  3. In a chat, tap + › Connectors and switch on Early Bird, then ask for a table.
  4. The first time it books, Claude asks you to sign in to Early Bird and tap Allow.

Claude's free plan allows one custom connector; paid plans allow more.

OptionalLet your assistant book for you

Searching and booking links work straight away. To let the assistant book and pay the booking fee:

  1. In the Early Bird app or on eatearlybird.com, go to Profile › AI bookings.
  2. Add a card and switch AI bookings on. Set the largest party and the highest fee it may pay.
  3. Your assistant always shows you the restaurant, time, party and fee, and waits for your yes before it books.

Switch AI bookings off, remove your card or disconnect an assistant any time in Profile › AI bookings, and cancel bookings as usual. With AI bookings off, the assistant gives you a link that opens Early Bird at the booking step.
