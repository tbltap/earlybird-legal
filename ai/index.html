Early Bird / Claude and ChatGPT

Book Early Bird tables from Claude or ChatGPT

Add Early Bird to your AI assistant and ask for a table: it finds 50%-off-food restaurants in Los Angeles and New York, checks open times and books for you.

Add it

Claude

  1. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector (or find Early Bird in the connector directory).
  2. Name: Early Bird. URL: https://xopcdblqmmrvyocqomqz.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
  3. In a chat, turn Early Bird on from the tools menu.

ChatGPT

  1. Settings → Apps & Connectors → Developer mode → Create, with the same URL.

What it can do

  • Search deals by city, neighbourhood, cuisine, date and party size.
  • Restaurant details: address, hours, deal terms and the booking fee.
  • Open times for up to 7 days.
  • Start a booking: a link that opens Early Bird's checkout at that time.
  • Book a table for you, if you allow it (below).

Let it book for you

  1. In the Early Bird app go to Profile → AI bookings, add a card and switch AI bookings on. You can set the largest party and the highest booking fee it may pay.
  2. When your assistant first books, it asks you to sign in to Early Bird and allow it.
  3. It confirms the restaurant, time, party and fee with you, books, and gives you your booking code.

Without AI bookings switched on, it gives you a link to finish the booking in Early Bird. Your card number is never shared with the assistant, and payment never happens in the chat. Switch AI bookings off or remove the card in the app at any time; cancel bookings in the app or on the web as usual.

Privacy

Searching needs no account and sends Early Bird only what you ask for (city, date, party size). When you sign in, the assistant acts as you within your Early Bird account. See the privacy policy. Questions: support@eatearlybird.com.
