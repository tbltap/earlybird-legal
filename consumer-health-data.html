Early Bird Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026.

This policy is about consumer health data: personal information that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which covers everything else. It applies to everyone who uses Early Bird, and it's written for Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's similar law.

1. What we collect

The only health-related information we collect is what you choose to tell us:

  • dietary needs, for example vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or halal, that you pick in your taste profile or on a booking;
  • food allergies you type in your taste profile or on a booking;
  • anything about a diet or an allergy that you write in a booking request or note.

We don't work out anything about your health from what you book, view or eat, and we don't collect any other health information. What Early Bird learns from what you do, for recommendations, never includes your diet, allergies or anything about your health.

2. Where it comes from

Only from you, in the Early Bird app or on https://eatearlybird.com. You choose whether to add them. We use them only to provide what you ask for: your booking and your recommendations.

3. Why we use it

  • to tell the restaurant you book, so it can prepare for your visit;
  • to leave out restaurants and dishes that don't suit you in your recommendations.

We never use it for advertising and never sell it.

4. Who we share it with

  • The restaurant you book, for that booking only. It sees the dietary needs and allergies on that booking, not your taste profile, and they're not added to its customer list.
  • Our service providers that host and run Early Bird for us (for example our database and hosting provider), only to provide the service, under contracts that limit their use of it.
  • When the law requires, for example a valid court order.

We don't share it with anyone else, and we don't sell it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Dietary needs and allergies on a booking, and anything you write in a booking request or note: deleted 30 days after the booking time.
  • In your taste profile: until you clear them in the app or delete your account.
  • Deleting your account deletes all of them.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • see the consumer health data we have about you and the list of everyone we shared it with;
  • stop us using it at any time, by clearing your dietary needs and allergies in the app (your account info). That also removes them from upcoming bookings;
  • delete it, in the app or by deleting your account (https://eatearlybird.com/account-deletion).

To ask us, email privacy@eatearlybird.com with "Consumer health data" in the subject. We answer within 45 days. If we refuse, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we'll reply within 45 days. If you're not satisfied with the appeal, you can contact your state's Attorney General (in Washington, the Washington State Attorney General's office).
