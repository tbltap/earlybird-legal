Early Bird Credit Terms

Effective 23 November 2026.

These terms explain Early Bird credit, the credit balance in your Early Bird account: how you earn it, how it is used and when it expires. In these terms, "credit" means Early Bird credit. They form part of the Early Bird Terms of Service. Early Bird is run by Ngassa Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company.

1. What Early Bird credit is

Early Bird credit is a balance in your Early Bird account that can be used only toward Early Bird booking fees, in the app or on the website. It can't be used at restaurants. It is a promotional and goodwill credit, not money:

  • it has no cash value and is never paid out or refunded as cash;
  • you can't buy it, and you can't transfer, sell, gift or exchange it, including between your own accounts;
  • it expires (§5);
  • it isn't a bank account, deposit, gift card or stored-value product, and doesn't earn interest.

Your balance and activity, including what is pending and when each amount expires, are in the app under Earn. The website shows your balance under Account.

3. Pending and available credit

Some credit is pending before you can use it, for example:

  • a review reward for a visit that wasn't checked in, until 48 hours after the table time (a review of a checked-in visit is rewarded when you send it);
  • a review reward while its photos are being checked, or while the review is held after someone reported it, until we decide.

Pending credit is shown separately and can't be spent. It becomes available automatically, or is cancelled if the condition isn't met (for example, you cancelled the booking, it was marked a no-show, or the photos weren't approved, in which case the lower review amount applies).

4. How credit is used

Only on booking fees. Credit can be used only toward Early Bird booking fees, in the app or on the website. It can't be used at restaurants, for food, drinks or tips, or to get cash back.

There are two kinds of credit, and they are used differently:

  • Returned credit is credit that comes back to you from a fee you paid: cancellation credit (Terms of Service §7), and a payment or refund we give back to you as credit. It is used first on your next booking fee, whatever the amount. If it covers only part of the fee, the rest is paid by card.
  • Earned credit is all other credit: booking and review rewards, restaurant offers, birthday rewards, credit from voucher codes and invitations, and promotions and goodwill credit from Early Bird. It is used only when it covers the whole fee, together with any returned credit. If it doesn't, it isn't used: it stays in your account, and the fee (less any returned credit) is paid by card.

For example, with a $4.00 booking fee: if you have $3.00 of rewards and nothing else, the rewards stay in your account and $4.00 is charged. If you also have $1.00 of cancellation credit, your credit covers the whole fee and nothing is charged. If you have $1.00 of cancellation credit and $2.00 of rewards, the $1.00 is used, the rewards stay, and $3.00 is charged.

The booking screen shows how much credit is used and what is charged before you pay. Within each kind, the amount that expires soonest is used first. If you cancel the booking, the credit used comes back under the cancellation policy as if you had paid it by card.

Free booking fee passes. A voucher code or an invitation can give you a free booking fee pass: your next booking fee is free, up to the amount shown. A pass isn't a balance, isn't money and covers one booking. It is used when your table is held, before any credit. If the fee is more than the pass covers, the rest is paid with credit under the rules above, and then by card. If you cancel that booking while the cancellation policy still gives something back, or the booking is refunded, you get the pass back. A no-show or a cancellation on the day of the booking uses it up. Cancellation credit and refunds are worked out on what you actually paid, never on the part the pass covered.

5. Expiry

Each amount of credit has its own expiry date, shown in the app:

Credit Expires
Cancellation credit 30 days after it is added
Booking, review and birthday rewards, promotions, credit from voucher codes and invitations, and free booking fee passes 90 days after it becomes available
Restaurant offers on the date the offer says, or 30 days after it becomes available if it doesn't say
Goodwill credit from Early Bird (for example after a problem with a booking) 180 days after it is added

Expired credit is removed and can't be brought back. We'll show credit that expires in the next 7 days in the app.

6. When credit is removed

We may remove or reverse credit:

  • when you cancel a booking that earned a booking reward before 23 November 2026 (all of it while pending; once available, only what is still unspent);
  • when a review or photo that earned a reward is removed for breaking the Terms of Service or our review rules, or turns out not to be about your own visit (if the reward was already available, it is taken back). We never remove a reward because of your score or what you wrote;
  • when an invitation reward is cancelled because your friend's booking was refunded (§2);
  • when credit was added by mistake or through a technical error;
  • when it was earned through fraud or abuse (§7).

We never take your balance below zero. If you have already spent some of the credit we remove, we remove only what is left.

7. Fraud and abuse

Running more than one account, using someone else's phone number, inviting yourself, booking and cancelling to collect rewards, posting reviews or photos of visits that didn't happen, colluding with a restaurant, using automated tools, or trying to sell or transfer credit is fraud. If we find it, we may remove the credit involved, cancel bookings, and suspend or close the accounts involved. We'll tell you and give you a chance to respond, unless that would help the fraud continue.

8. Closing your account

If you delete your account, or we close it for breaking our terms, your credit, available and pending, and any free booking fee pass are lost and can't be refunded or moved. A friend who deletes their account loses their pass and the invitation.

9. Changes and ending the programme

We may change how credit is earned (for example the amounts or limits) for the future at any time; credit you already have keeps its expiry date. We'll give at least 30 days' notice in the app before any change that reduces credit you already have or how you can use it. If we end the credit programme, we'll give at least 60 days' notice, during which you can still spend your available credit.
