How it works

Restaurants have quiet tables early in the evening. You take them, and the restaurant takes half off the food.

  1. Find an early table

    Browse restaurants near you and pick a day, a time and how many of you are coming.

  2. Book in the app

    Pay a small booking fee per diner to hold the table. You get a booking code.

  3. Show your code

    Order as normal, show your code, and your food bill is 50% off. Drinks are full price.

Plans change? Cancel in the app and get credit back under our cancellation policy.

Own a restaurant?

Listing your early tables on Early Bird is free. Fill quiet hours with new guests who come to eat.
