Our grandparents had it right.

The early-bird special was never a compromise: a quieter room, a kitchen with time for you, and a better price. We just brought it back to the restaurants you'd want to eat at.

How it works

  1. Pick an early table

    Browse restaurants near you and choose a day, time and party size.

  2. Book it

    Pay a small booking fee per diner to hold the table. You get a booking code.

  3. Eat for half

    Show your code when you order. The food is 50% off; drinks are full price.
