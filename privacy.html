Early Bird Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal information Early Bird collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. Early Bird is run by Ngassa Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, doing business as Early Bird ("Early Bird", "we", "us"), 11644 S Brisbane Dr, Sandy, Utah 84094, United States. Questions: privacy@eatearlybird.com.

It covers the Early Bird app, the website at https://eatearlybird.com (including booking on the website) and Early Bird accounts. It doesn't cover what a restaurant does with information you choose to give it (§7): that restaurant's own privacy policy applies.

1. The short version

  • Diet and allergies. If you tell us about a diet or a food allergy, our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy covers how we use it.
  • No cross-app tracking. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, we don't use advertising SDKs or analytics on the website, and we don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • No card numbers. Stripe processes your payments. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Restaurants get your contact details only when you agree, one restaurant at a time. A restaurant sees what it needs to serve your booking. When you book on the website, the box to share your contact details with that restaurant is ticked, and you can untick it before you book; in the app it's a switch on the restaurant's page, off unless you turn it on. Getting a restaurant's offers is always off unless you tick it yourself. You can turn either off at any time (§7).
  • We use what you do in Early Bird to personalise it. Your bookings, taste profile, receipts, reviews, saves, and when and where you use Early Bird help us recommend restaurants, dishes, deals and offers, and work out what you like (§3). This stays inside Early Bird; we don't use data from other companies' apps or websites.
  • Location when you allow it. We use your device's exact location to filter places, deals and offers near you and to match you to offers for an area. We keep the general area (for example the city or neighbourhood), not your exact position.
  • You can delete your account in the app, and with it your personal information (§10).

On the website. The website stores a few things in your browser so it works:

  • your sign-in, so you stay signed in (see below);
  • the random device ID described above;
  • while you book, the booking you're making, which is cleared when you close the tab.

When you sign in on eatearlybird.com, we keep you signed in by saving your sign-in on your device in your browser's storage. It stays there until you sign out. Signing out removes it from your device. We don't use it for tracking or advertising. If you use a shared computer, sign out when you're done.

Links in our posts and ads. Links we share on social media, in ads and elsewhere look like eatearlybird.com/go/<name>. When you open one, in the app or on the website, we record which link it was, when, the random device ID described above, whether it opened in the app or the website, the kind of device (iPhone, Android or computer) and the name of the site you came from (for example instagram.com). If you then sign up or book within 30 days, we count that for the link. We use this only to see which of our posts and ads bring diners. It stays with us, isn't linked to other companies' data and isn't used to show you ads; we don't record your IP address or browser details for it. The link itself carries nothing about you.

Counting visits. When you open the app, or a page of our website, we count the visit: when, the same random device ID, app or website, the kind of device, the part of the site (for example the booking page) and the name of the site you came from. We count at most one visit per device every 30 minutes and use the totals only to see how many people use Early Bird and where they find us. We don't record your IP address or browser details for it, don't use cookies or other companies' trackers for it, and it isn't used to show you ads.

The website uses no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking pixels. Its pages and fonts are served from our own site. Two parts come from other companies. The checkout loads Stripe's payment code (js.stripe.com), which may set its own cookies, such as __stripe_mid and __stripe_sid, to prevent fraud (see Stripe's cookie policy). An address link opens Google Maps if you tap it. You can clear what the website stores at any time in your browser's settings; you'll then need to sign in again.

Your taste profile and booking details. Answering taste questions and adding booking details is optional. Learning from what you do in Early Bird is part of how Early Bird works and is always on, but it never includes your diet, allergies or anything about your health. You can see, change and delete your taste profile, including what we've learned, any time in the app, and you can turn off "Show recommendations".

We don't collect contacts, precise location history, browsing history from other apps or sites, or audio. We don't ask for health information. If you choose to tell us about a diet or a food allergy (in your taste profile or on a booking), we keep only what you type or pick, use it only for your bookings and recommendations, and share it only with the restaurant you book, for that booking. You choose whether to add them, in your account info or on a booking; our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains how we use them. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo (for a review or to scan a receipt). Sign-in codes are sent and checked by our text message provider; we don't keep them.

3. How we use it

  • To run Early Bird: create and secure your account, sign you in, show restaurants and deals, hold and confirm bookings, take payments, cancel and refund, run your credit balance, publish your reviews, and give you support.
  • For rewards and offers: work out booking, review, birthday and restaurant rewards, and whether a review qualifies (for example, that you had a checked-in booking there); find and choose offers from restaurants that suit you, show them to you in Early Bird, and count what they brought.
  • For safety and fraud prevention: stop fake accounts, fake reviews, booking abuse and payment fraud; enforce our terms; keep Early Bird secure.
  • For recommendations and personalisation: suggest restaurants, dishes, deals and offers from your taste profile (§2) and what we learn from what you do in Early Bird, including the times, days and areas you book, search and eat. Dietary needs and allergies are used only to leave out what doesn't suit you, never for advertising (our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy). If you turn off "Show recommendations", we stop showing you personalised suggestions.
  • To understand what you like and what works: infer your interests and habits (for example the cuisines, price range, times and areas you prefer), and link visits, bookings, receipts and spending to the offers, deals, posts, featured listings and features that led to them.
  • To give restaurants insights: show restaurants information about their guests and how their deals and offers did, in totals or in other forms that don't identify you (§7). Your contact details and history go to a restaurant only as §7 says.
  • To run and grow Early Bird: understand how Early Bird is used, fix problems, build and test new features, decide which restaurants and areas to add, and plan, measure and report on the business, using your information as this policy describes and in totals or other forms that don't identify you.
  • To build our own models: use your information (never your diet, allergies or anything about your health) to build, train and improve our own recommendation, matching, offer and other machine-learning models. Outside AI providers don't train their models on it (§7).
  • For location-based offers: use your exact location at the time to filter what you see and to match you to offers that restaurants or we set for an area or time window, and keep the general area to plan and measure them.
  • For featured restaurants: choose which featured (paid) restaurants to show, based on the city, area or location you picked for the search and your interests in Early Bird, and count how many people saw and tapped them (§6).
  • To send messages: sign-in codes, booking confirmations and receipts; Early Bird offers as app notifications if you allow notifications; and Early Bird news and offers by email if you agree (§8).
  • For legal reasons: tax, accounting, responding to lawful requests, and defending legal claims.

4. Payments

When you pay, your card, Apple Pay or Google Pay details go straight from the app or the website's checkout to Stripe, Inc., which processes the payment as our payment provider. Stripe also uses payment information to prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). With Apple Pay or Google Pay, Apple or Google handles your card details under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment result and the information in §2.

Booking from an AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT)

You can add Early Bird to an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT. Without signing in, the assistant can only search restaurants, deals and open times, the same as anyone on our website, and give you a link to book on Early Bird.

Signing in. If you sign in to Early Bird from the assistant, you see which app is asking and choose whether to allow it. The app you allow gets a sign-in for your account. It can then see your name and phone number (to put them on a booking), whether AI bookings is on, your card's brand and last four digits, your booking limits, and the bookings it makes. What you and the assistant say to each other, and what it does with our answers, is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy, not this one. You can disconnect an assistant at any time in the app or on eatearlybird.com (Profile → AI bookings), which ends its access.

AI bookings (off unless you turn it on). If you switch on AI bookings and save a card in the app or on eatearlybird.com, an assistant you signed in can book a table and pay the booking fee with that card, within the limits you set (party size and fee per booking) and limits we set (at most 3 bookings a day, 1 per restaurant a day and $100 of booking fees a week). Before it books, it must show you the restaurant, time, party and highest fee and ask you to confirm. Your card details go straight to Stripe, which keeps the card for us; we keep only Stripe's references to it and what the app shows ("Visa •• 4242"). We record that a booking was made through an assistant, and which one. Bookings made this way are ordinary Early Bird bookings: you can see and cancel them in the app or on the website, with the usual cancellation terms.

Turning it off. Switch off AI bookings or remove the card in the app or on eatearlybird.com at any time; removing it also deletes it at Stripe. If you delete your account, the saved card is deleted at Stripe too.

5. Text messages

If you sign in with your phone number, we use our text message provider (Twilio) to send you one-time sign-in codes. Your phone number and the message go to that provider only to deliver the message. We don't send marketing texts. We don't sell, rent or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. This doesn't cover the providers that deliver messages for us (section 7). Reply STOP to any text to stop them.

6. Featured restaurants and measurement

Restaurants can pay to be featured in Early Bird. Featured listings are labelled. We choose them using the place you're searching, what we know of your interests in Early Bird (§3) and rotation, never a profile of you built from other apps or websites. We record that a featured listing was shown or tapped on a device (at most once per device per day per placement) and give restaurants totals only (views, taps, bookings). This information stays inside Early Bird and isn't combined with data from other companies, so under Apple's definition it isn't tracking.

7. Who we share it with

Restaurants you book. To serve your booking, the restaurant sees your first name, the number of diners, the date and time, your booking code and anything you add to that booking: the occasion, dietary needs, allergies, seating preference and note. It sees these on that booking only. They're not added to its customer list, and it doesn't see your taste profile; it may see information about its guests in totals or other forms that don't identify you (§3). It learns that you visited when it checks you in. Restaurants also see reviews you write about them.

Restaurants you choose to share with. We ask you per restaurant, with two separate choices:

  • Sharing your contact details. When you book on the website, the booking page has a box "Share my name, mobile number and email with <restaurant>, so they can contact me about my bookings". It's ticked, and you can untick it before you book. In the app, sharing is a switch on the restaurant's page, off unless you turn it on. If you share, that restaurant has your name, mobile number, email, your birthday month if you added a birthday, and your history with it (visits, bookings, reviews, favourites) in its customer list, for that booking and after.
  • Offers. A separate box, "Let <restaurant> send me offers through Early Bird", is unticked, and only you can tick it, on the website or in the app. It lets that restaurant send you offers (for example a comeback, birthday or "come try us" reward) in the app and by email. Separately, we may show you restaurants' offers inside Early Bird that we think suit you, without that box. We never treat a box we ticked for you as agreeing to a restaurant's offers.

You can turn either off at any time in the app (Profile → Privacy → Restaurants) or by emailing privacy@eatearlybird.com. The restaurant then loses access in Early Bird and must stop using your contact details for marketing (our Restaurant Terms require this). If you share neither, a restaurant sees only what it needs for your booking and totals that don't identify you.

8. Marketing

Early Bird news and offers by email. We may ask when you book whether you'd like them, and you can say no. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Booking and account messages aren't marketing and you'll still get those. Offers from a restaurant need your separate yes for that restaurant (§7).

10. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • see and correct your details in the app (Profile), or ask us for a copy of your information;
  • delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or at https://eatearlybird.com/account-deletion;
  • see, change and delete your taste profile, including what we've learned, in the app;
  • turn off location access in your device's settings, "Show recommendations", marketing emails, and any restaurant's access to your contact details;
  • ask us questions or complain at privacy@eatearlybird.com.

Photos of restaurants. Restaurant listings show photos of the restaurant, some taken from the restaurant's own website. If you can be recognised in one and want it removed, email privacy@eatearlybird.com and we'll remove it promptly, within 7 days.

We'll answer within 30 days (45 days for California requests, §12). We may need to confirm it's you, usually by a code sent to your phone or email on the account.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls (every table in our database is protected by row-level rules; staff and restaurant access is limited to what they need), and payment processing by a PCI-certified provider. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

13. Notice of financial incentive (California)

Early Bird credit rewards (Credit Terms) are a financial incentive under the CCPA, because some of them involve personal information:

  • Booking rewards and restaurant offers use your account and booking history.
  • Birthday rewards use the birthday you choose to add.
  • Review rewards use your review, photos and booking history.
  • Restaurant comeback and "come try us" offers need you to agree to share your details with that restaurant (§7).
  • Invite a friend and voucher codes use your account, device and phone number, and your friend's, to check that a code is used once and that you didn't invite yourself.

You opt in by creating an account, adding a birthday, writing a review, or agreeing to a restaurant's request, and you can withdraw at any time by removing your birthday, deleting the review, turning off the restaurant's access, or deleting your account (unspent rewards tied to what you withdraw are removed). We estimate the value of your information as roughly equal to the rewards we give for it: the amounts in the Credit Terms (for example $0.25 for a review, up to $0.50 with photos), based on what it costs us to give them.

14. Other US states

If you live in a state with a consumer privacy law (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Oregon, Texas or New Jersey), you have similar rights to access, correct, delete and get a copy of your information, and to appeal our decision by replying to our answer. We don't sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects. We use your taste profile and what we infer from your activity in Early Bird to personalise it (§3); because that uses only what you do in Early Bird, it isn't targeted advertising. You can turn off "Show recommendations".

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada and similar laws). Dietary needs and allergies you give us may be "consumer health data". How we collect, use and share them, and your rights, are in our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

15. Children

Early Bird is not for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact privacy@eatearlybird.com and we'll delete it.

16. Where we store information

We're based in the United States and store information in the United States. If you use Early Bird from elsewhere, your information is processed in the US.

17. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example a new use or a new kind of sharing, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it applies and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.
