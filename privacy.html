Early Bird Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal information Early Bird collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. Early Bird is run by Ngassa Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Early Bird", "we", "us"), 11644 S Brisbane Dr, Sandy, Utah 84094, United States. Questions: privacy@venuu.menu.

It covers the Early Bird app, https://eatearlybird.com and Early Bird accounts. It doesn't cover what a restaurant does with information you choose to give it (§7): that restaurant's own privacy policy applies.

1. The short version

  • No cross-app tracking. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, we don't use advertising SDKs, and we don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • No card numbers. Stripe processes your payments. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Restaurants get your contact details only if you say yes, one restaurant at a time. A restaurant sees what it needs to serve your booking. Joining its customer list or getting its offers is a separate choice, off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Location only when you ask. We use your device's location to show places near you, when you use that feature. We don't store your precise location.
  • You can delete your account in the app, and with it your personal information (§10).

3. How we use it

  • To run Early Bird: create and secure your account, sign you in, show restaurants and deals, hold and confirm bookings, take payments, cancel and refund, run your credit balance, publish your reviews, and give you support.
  • For rewards and offers: work out booking, review, birthday and restaurant rewards, and whether a review qualifies (for example, that you had a checked-in booking there).
  • For safety and fraud prevention: stop fake accounts, fake reviews, booking abuse and payment fraud; enforce our terms; keep Early Bird secure.
  • To improve Early Bird: understand, in aggregate, how features are used and fix problems.
  • For featured restaurants: choose which featured (paid) restaurants to show, based on the city, area or location you picked for the search, and count how many people saw and tapped them (§6).
  • To send messages you asked for: sign-in codes, booking confirmations and receipts, and, only if you opt in, Early Bird news and offers.
  • For legal reasons: tax, accounting, responding to lawful requests, and defending legal claims.

4. Payments

When you pay, your card or Apple Pay details go straight from the app to Stripe, Inc., which processes the payment as our payment provider. Stripe also uses payment information to prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). With Apple Pay, Apple handles your card details under Apple's privacy policy. We receive only the payment result and the information in §2.

5. Text messages

We use our text message provider to send you sign-in codes. Your phone number and the message go to that provider only to deliver the message. We don't send marketing texts.

6. Featured restaurants and measurement

Restaurants can pay to be featured in Early Bird. Featured listings are labelled. We choose them using the place you're searching and random rotation, not a profile of you built from other apps or websites. We record that a featured listing was shown or tapped on a device (at most once per device per day per placement) and give restaurants totals only (views, taps, bookings). This information stays inside Early Bird and isn't combined with data from other companies, so under Apple's definition it isn't tracking.

7. Who we share it with

Restaurants you book. To serve your booking, the restaurant sees your first name, the number of diners, the date and time, your booking code and any note you add, and whether you paid with credit. It learns that you visited when it checks you in. Restaurants also see reviews you write about them.

Restaurants you choose to share with. A restaurant can ask to add you to its customer list or send you offers (for example a comeback, birthday or "come try us" reward). We ask you per restaurant, and it's off unless you turn it on. If you say yes, that restaurant can see your name, contact details (phone and/or email, as the request says) and your history with it (visits, bookings, reviews, favourites, birthday month if you added a birthday), and can send you offers. You can turn it off at any time in Profile → Privacy → Restaurants; the restaurant then loses access in Early Bird and must stop using your contact details for marketing (our Restaurant Terms require this). Without your yes, a restaurant sees only totals that don't identify you.

The public. Your reviews and review photos are public, with your first name and last initial.

8. Marketing

Early Bird news and offers by email are off unless you opt in (Profile → Settings). Every email has an unsubscribe link. Booking and account messages aren't marketing and you'll still get those.

10. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • see and correct your details in the app (Profile), or ask us for a copy of your information;
  • delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or at https://eatearlybird.com/account-deletion;
  • turn off location access in your device's settings, marketing emails, and any restaurant's access to your contact details;
  • ask us questions or complain at privacy@venuu.menu.

We'll answer within 30 days (45 days for California requests, §12). We may need to confirm it's you, usually by a code sent to your phone or email on the account.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls (every table in our database is protected by row-level rules; staff and restaurant access is limited to what they need), and payment processing by a PCI-certified provider. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

13. Notice of financial incentive (California)

Early Bird credit rewards (Credit Terms) are a financial incentive under the CCPA, because some of them involve personal information:

  • Booking rewards and restaurant offers use your account and booking history.
  • Birthday rewards use the birthday you choose to add.
  • Review rewards use your review, photos and booking history.
  • Restaurant comeback and "come try us" offers need you to agree to share your details with that restaurant (§7).

You opt in by creating an account, adding a birthday, writing a review, or agreeing to a restaurant's request, and you can withdraw at any time by removing your birthday, deleting the review, turning off the restaurant's access, or deleting your account (unspent rewards tied to what you withdraw are removed). We estimate the value of your information as roughly equal to the rewards we give for it: the amounts in the Credit Terms (for example $0.50 per booking), based on what it costs us to give them.

14. Other US states

If you live in a state with a consumer privacy law (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Oregon, Texas or New Jersey), you have similar rights to access, correct, delete and get a copy of your information, and to appeal our decision by replying to our answer. We don't sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for profiling that has legal effects.

15. Children

Early Bird is not for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact privacy@venuu.menu and we'll delete it.

16. Where we store information

We're based in the United States and store information in the United States. If you use Early Bird from elsewhere, your information is processed in the US.

17. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example a new use or a new kind of sharing, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it applies and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.
