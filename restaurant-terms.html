  • Keep your menu, prices, hours and deal details accurate and up to date.

3. Your deal

  • The discount. Your deal is a percentage off the food bill (50% unless you set a different percentage for a time slot), for the diners in the booking, at the booked time, dining in. Drinks, tax, tips and service charges aren't discounted unless you say so.
  • Deal menu. You may limit the deal to a deal menu, but it must be fair: it must include at least 5 mains (or at least a third of your mains, rounded up, if you have fewer than 15), and the average price of the mains on it must be at least 85% of the average price of all your mains. Early Bird admins may approve an exception for a recorded reason. If your menu changes so the deal menu is no longer fair, update it.
  • Order rules. You may set up to 4 order rules per person (for example "1 drink per person", "1 side or dessert per person"). They're shown to diners before they book. A non-alcoholic drink must always count as a drink. You can't add other conditions at the table.
  • Times and tables. You choose the meals, days, times and number of tables. You can pause, close or change slots in advance. Bookings already confirmed must be honoured.

4. Honouring bookings

  • Check the diner's booking code (scan the QR or type the code) and give the discount when the diners follow the order rules. Checking in the code is how a visit is recorded.
  • If you can't honour a confirmed booking (for example you close unexpectedly), cancel it in Early Bird as early as possible; the diner gets their booking fee back.
  • If you refuse a valid booking or the discount, we'll refund the diner's booking fee and may charge you that amount on your next statement and, if it happens again, pause or remove your listing.

5. Ads and featured placements

  • The one-table-per-night commitment. To run ads or featured placements, you must offer at least one bookable Early Bird table every night of the week at any meal. We check this live. If a night is missing, your ads pause automatically and Early Bird tells you which night to add; they restart when it's fixed. Paused days aren't charged.
  • Budget. You set a monthly ad budget for a calendar month (in your restaurant's time zone), for the current month or up to 2 months ahead, and choose placements (for example Home, Search, Discover). You can pause at any time.
  • How spend works. The budget is spread evenly over the days of the month. A day is charged the first time your ad is shown that day; days your ad doesn't run cost nothing. Spend never goes over your budget. You can't lower a budget below what has already been spent that month or below twice the credit you've already accepted that month.
  • Results. We show you views, taps and bookings. We don't guarantee any number of views, taps, bookings or diners.
  • Labelling and content. Ads and featured placements are labelled as such in the app. Your ad content must be accurate and lawful, and we may refuse or remove ads that aren't.

6. Accepting Early Bird credit

You can choose to accept Early Bird credit from diners as part payment of their bill.

  • When. Only while your ads are running in that month (a budget is set, ads aren't paused, and the table commitment is met).
  • How much. Up to 50% of that month's ad budget. Once you reach it, you stop accepting credit for the rest of the month. Unused room doesn't roll over to the next month.
  • How. The diner shows a 6-digit code from their booking. Your staff enter it in Early Bird, which confirms the amount and takes it from the diner's balance. Take exactly that amount off the diner's bill. You can reverse a mistaken acceptance in the same month.
  • What you get. Every dollar of credit you accept is credited against your ad spend on that month's statement: your bill is ad spend minus credit accepted, and never below $0. Credit isn't cash to you: it's never paid out, and it can't reduce anything other than your Early Bird ad bill.
  • Fair treatment. Don't charge diners more, or refuse service, because they pay with credit.

7. Offers you fund

You can create offers for diners (for example "come try us", comeback, birthday and win-back rewards). You set the reward, the budget, the limit per diner and the dates. Rewards are given to diners as Early Bird credit, and the cost of rewards redeemed is added to your monthly statement, up to the offer's budget. Offers to a customer list go only to diners who have agreed to hear from you (§9). Honour every offer you publish until it ends.

8. Statements and payment

  • Payment. We send a Stripe invoice, due within 15 days. Amounts are in US dollars and don't include taxes, which you pay where they apply.
  • Questions. Tell us within 30 days of a statement if you think it's wrong. We'll correct mistakes.
  • Late payment. If a payment fails or is late, we may pause your ads and paid tools until it's paid.
  • Paid tools. Retention tools (customer lists, segments, messages, exports) are part of paid plans (Growth or Pro), priced as shown in Early Bird or in an order form, billed monthly on the same statement.
