Cobi's At Dhaba

2104 Main Street, Santa Monica, CA 90405
Breakfast & brunch

Cobi’s is a restaurant offering South Asian dishes in Santa Monica, imagined by Cobi Marsh. Cobi has created a space that combines her passions of food, design and music into a singular hospitality experience. An offering of curry, Asian street food and Soul and Reggae beats in an atmosphere full of texture and character.
