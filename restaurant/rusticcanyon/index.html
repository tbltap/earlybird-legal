Rustic Canyon

1119 Wilshire Blvd, Santa Monica, CA 90401
American, Wine & cocktails

We're a critically-acclaimed, neighborhood restaurant that’s a true reflection of incredible, regional farmers and purveyors. The hyper-seasonal menu changes daily and embodies James Beard-nominated Chef/Owner Jeremy Fox’s slow-food ethos. Everything’s made from scratch and creatively draws upon a vast larder, which extends to Bar Manager Justin Dicken’s inventive, market-driven cocktail program. Additionally, Rustic Canyon Family Wine Director Kathryn Coker curates an award-winning, 300+ wine list showcasing small, planet-friendly producers whom she knows and loves.  *A 4% charge is added by the restaurant to all checks to help offer comprehensive health care to all employees. Please let us know if you have any questions. Thank you for supporting a healthier staff.
