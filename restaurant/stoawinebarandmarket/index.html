wine bar

Stoa is more than a wine bar; it’s a celebration of life’s finer moments, those meaningful experiences that disguise themselves in the mundane. Inspired by the principles of Stoicism, we embrace simplicity, intentionality, and the joy of savoring the present. Our curated selection of artisanal cheeses and charcuterie invites you to indulge your senses—whether on a beautifully crafted board, in a panino, or atop a Roman-style pizza. Stoa is where Marina’s community comes together, a relaxed gathering spot to unwind with friends, debate deep questions, and enjoy the coastal breeze over a thoughtfully selected bottle of wine.
