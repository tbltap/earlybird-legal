Vui Vẻ <voo-vay> means to have fun & be happy. We’re a fast-casual Vietnamese eatery serving comforting, craveable favorites — phở, bánh mì, rice plates, vermicelli bowls, spring rolls, Vietnamese coffee & house drinks. Find us in Brentwood, Little Tokyo, North Hollywood.
