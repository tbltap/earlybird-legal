FAQ

Before you put a table live.

Who pays Early Bird?

For the core listing, diners pay Early Bird’s booking fee. The restaurant does not pay Early Bird a sign-up, monthly listing, per-booking or per-diner fee. Optional paid tools are separate.

What gets discounted?

The standard offer is 50% off food. Drinks, tax, tips and service charges are separate unless the restaurant expressly chooses otherwise.

Can we pause the offer?

You can change or pause future availability. A pause does not cancel confirmed bookings; those still need to be honoured.

Does Early Bird guarantee bookings?

No. The product helps diners discover and book available tables, but the number of views, taps, bookings and diners can vary.

What happens before we go live?

You find or add the restaurant, verify that you’re authorised to act for it, set the offer, add the listing details and submit for review.
