Early Bird text messages

Early Bird is run by Ngassa Holdings LLC, doing business as Early Bird.

What we send

Only one-time sign-in codes, when you sign in to Early Bird with your phone number (in the Early Bird iPhone app or at eatearlybird.com/book), or confirm a change to your account. One message each time you ask for a code. No marketing texts.

Example: “Your Early Bird code is 123456. Don't share it with anyone.”

How you agree

You type your own mobile number on the Early Bird sign-in screen and tap Next. We then send a one-time sign-in code to that number by SMS text message. Right under the number, the screen says: “Early Bird texts 1 sign-in code per request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. By continuing you agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy,” with links to both.

Stopping texts and getting help

Reply STOP to any Early Bird text to stop them, and HELP for help. You can still sign in with email. Message and data rates may apply. Help: support@eatearlybird.com or eatearlybird.com/support.

We don't sell, rent or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. See the Terms of Service (section 4) and Privacy Policy (section 5).
