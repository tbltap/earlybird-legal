Early Bird Los Angeles

The good part of the night, now in LA.

Early Bird is now in Los Angeles. Local restaurants open their early and quieter tables, and when you book one through Early Bird you get 50% off the food.

Pick a neighbourhood, pick a time, show your code when you order. Drinks are full price; some places ask each guest to order a drink or a side at full price, and the restaurant's page says so before you book.
