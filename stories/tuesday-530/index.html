Why go now

Tuesday, 5:30

Nobody's here yet. That's the whole point.

Early on a weeknight the room is yours: the good booth, the chef's attention, no wait for a second round.

Restaurants would rather fill those tables than leave them empty, which is why they're happy to make it worth your while.
