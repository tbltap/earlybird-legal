Early Bird support

We're here to help with bookings, payments, Early Bird credit and your account, in the app or on the website.

Email: support@eatearlybird.com. We answer within 1 business day. In the app: Profile → Support → Contact us. Restaurants: support@eatearlybird.com.

Common questions

How does Early Bird work? Pick a restaurant, choose a meal, date, time and the number of diners, and pay a small booking fee per diner. At the restaurant, show your booking code and get the discount on the food (usually 50%). Drinks, tax and tips are full price.

What does the booking fee pay for? Early Bird's booking service. It's shown per diner before you pay, and it doesn't go to the restaurant.

Do I need an account to browse? Not in the app. On the website you sign in first, with your email and password. You need an account to book, earn credit and write reviews. In the app you can sign up with your phone number (we text you a code) or email.

Can I book on the website? Yes, at eatearlybird.com/book. Use the same account as the app. Your bookings and credit show in both. On the website you can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

How do I cancel? Open the booking (Bookings tab in the app, or My bookings on the website) and choose Cancel booking. Within 60 minutes of booking you get the whole fee back as Early Bird credit; before the day of the booking, half; on the day, nothing. See the Terms of Service.

The restaurant didn't honour my booking. Email support@eatearlybird.com within 7 days with your booking code. We'll refund your booking fee and follow up with the restaurant.

What is Early Bird credit? The credit balance in your Early Bird account. You get it back from some cancellations, and earn it from bookings, reviews and restaurant offers. It can be used only on Early Bird booking fees, not at restaurants. It has no cash value and it expires. See the Credit Terms.

When is my credit used? Credit you got back from a cancellation is used first on your next booking fee, whatever the amount. Credit you earned is used only when your credit covers the whole fee; otherwise it stays in your account and the fee is paid by card. The booking screen shows what is used and what is charged before you pay.

Why is a review marked as rewarded? When Early Bird gives credit for a review, we label it so other diners know. The reward is the same whatever score you give.

How do I report a review or photo? Tap ••• on the review and choose Report, or email support@eatearlybird.com.

I'm a restaurant or a photographer. How do I get a photo taken down? Email support@eatearlybird.com with the restaurant's name and which photo (a screenshot or link is fine). We'll remove it promptly, normally within 2 business days. You don't need a formal copyright notice. Restaurants that have joined Early Bird can also keep or remove their photos themselves. If you appear in a photo, you can ask the same way.

The restaurant charged me more because I booked with Early Bird. Restaurants agree to honour their deal as listed and not to charge Early Bird guests more. Email support@eatearlybird.com within 7 days with your booking code and we'll refund your booking fee.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or see Delete your account.

Is my card stored? No. Stripe processes payments; we never see your full card number.
