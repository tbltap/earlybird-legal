Early Bird support

We're here to help with bookings, payments, credit and your account.

Email: support@eatearlybird.com. We answer within 1 business day. In the app: Profile → Support → Contact us. Restaurants: support@eatearlybird.com.

Common questions

How does Early Bird work? Pick a restaurant, choose a meal, date, time and the number of diners, and pay a small booking fee per diner. At the restaurant, show your booking code and get the discount on the food (usually 50%). Drinks, tax and tips are full price.

What does the booking fee pay for? Early Bird's booking service. It's shown per diner before you pay, and it doesn't go to the restaurant.

Do I need an account to browse? No. You need one to book, earn credit and write reviews. Sign up with your phone number (we text you a code) or email.

How do I cancel? Open the booking in the Bookings tab and tap Cancel booking. Within 60 minutes of booking you get the whole fee back as Early Bird credit; before the day of the booking, half; on the day, nothing. See the Terms of Service.

The restaurant didn't honour my booking. Email support@eatearlybird.com within 7 days with your booking code. We'll refund your booking fee and follow up with the restaurant.

What is Early Bird credit? A balance you earn from bookings, some cancellations, reviews and restaurant offers. You can use it on booking fees and at restaurants that accept it. It has no cash value and it expires. See the Credit Terms.

How do I use credit at a restaurant? Open your booking, tap Use credit on your bill, pick an amount and show the 6-digit code to the staff within 15 minutes. Nothing is taken until they confirm it.

Why is a review marked as rewarded? When Early Bird gives credit for a review, we label it so other diners know. The reward is the same whatever score you give.

How do I report a review or photo? Tap ••• on the review and choose Report, or email support@eatearlybird.com.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or see Delete your account.

Is my card stored? No. Stripe processes payments; we never see your full card number.
