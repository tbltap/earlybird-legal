  1. Choose a deal. Each restaurant page shows the meals, days and times it offers, the discount, how many people a booking can include, and what each diner orders at full price (section 6).

  3. Booking fee. Early Bird charges a booking fee for each diner in the booking. The fee is shown before you pay, on the booking screen, with the total. It pays for Early Bird's booking service. It is not part of the restaurant's bill and it doesn't go to the restaurant.

  5. Confirmation. Once payment succeeds, your booking is confirmed and you get a booking code and QR code in the app, or a booking code on the website's booking page. Stripe may email you a receipt.
  6. At the restaurant. Arrive in the booked time window with the number of people booked and show your code. You pay the restaurant directly for your meal, at the discounted price, plus anything not covered by the deal. At a restaurant that isn't a partner yet there's no deal: you pay its normal prices.

If you're more than 15 minutes late, the deal is void: the restaurant doesn't have to seat you or give the discount, the booking is marked as missed and the booking fee isn't refunded. At a restaurant that isn't a partner yet the same time limit applies: more than 15 minutes late, it doesn't have to seat you, the booking is marked as missed and the booking fee isn't refunded. If you're running late, use On my way in the app to call the restaurant and let them know. On my way doesn't extend this time.

6. The discount and the rules for it

  • What it covers. The discount is 50% off food on the restaurant's menu, for the diners in your booking, eaten in, at the booked time.
  • What you order at full price. The restaurant may ask each person in your booking to order up to two items at full price: one drink, and/or one side, starter or dessert. The deal page and your booking show exactly which, before you book. The 50% discount is on your food; the required items, drinks, tax, tips and service charges are full price. Nothing else is required: there's no minimum spend, and no dish is left off the deal. A non-alcoholic drink counts as a drink. If your table doesn't order the required items, the restaurant may decline the discount.
  • One deal per booking. The discount can't be combined with other restaurant offers unless the restaurant says so.
  • The deal rules. Each deal comes with rules, shown on the restaurant page and the booking screen before you pay:
    • arrive on time: check in within 15 minutes of your booking time. More than 15 minutes late, the deal is void: the restaurant doesn't have to seat you or give the discount, the booking is missed and the fee isn't refunded (section 5);
    • the booked time window: order within the deal's time window;
    • party size: the discount is for the number of people you booked. For extra people the restaurant may charge full price or not seat them;
    • food only: the discount covers food only, not the items ordered at full price;
    • what you order at full price: each diner orders the required items, if any (above);
    • show your booking code before you order.
  • If you don't follow the deal rules. The restaurant may refuse the discount, for your whole table or for the part that doesn't meet the rules, and charge its normal prices. Your booking fee isn't refunded, and the fee-back promises below don't apply. Before you order, check the rules, and ask the restaurant if you're not sure, so the bill isn't a surprise.
  • The bill is between you and the restaurant. The restaurant sets its prices and charges you for your meal. Early Bird doesn't set or collect the restaurant's bill and isn't responsible for it (section 18). A question about the bill itself goes to the restaurant; if you think a restaurant didn't honour a deal you followed the rules for, tell us (below).
  • Tipping. Tipping is up to you. Many diners tip on the price before the discount.
  • If the restaurant doesn't honour the booking. Tell us within 7 days through the app or at support@eatearlybird.com. If the restaurant refused a valid booking, was closed, wouldn't give the discount when you followed the rules and arrived within 15 minutes of your booking time, or charged you more because you booked with Early Bird, we'll refund your booking fee to your card and follow it up with the restaurant. Restaurants that join Early Bird promise to honour their deal as written and not to charge Early Bird guests more.
  • Restaurants that aren't partners yet: our fee-back promise. A booking at a restaurant marked "not an Early Bird partner yet" comes with no discount. If you arrive within 15 minutes of your booking time and the restaurant doesn't honour your booking (for example it has no table for you, is closed, refuses the booking, or charges you more because you booked through Early Bird), we refund your whole booking fee, to your card or as Early Bird credit, whichever you choose. Tell us within 7 days of your booking time, in the app (Bookings → your booking → Get help) or at support@eatearlybird.com, with your booking code. You don't need to show a receipt.
