5. Booking a table and the booking fee

  1. Choose a deal. Each restaurant page shows the meals, days and times it offers, the discount, how many people a booking can include, and any order rules (section 6).
  2. Hold. When you pick a time, we hold the table for a few minutes while you pay.
  3. Booking fee. Early Bird charges a booking fee for each diner in the booking. The fee is shown before you pay, on the booking screen, with the total. It pays for Early Bird's booking service. It is not part of the restaurant's bill and it doesn't go to the restaurant.
  4. Payment. In the app you pay by card or Apple Pay. On the website you pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or another payment method shown at checkout. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. If you have Early Bird credit, it can pay some or all of the fee: credit returned to you from a cancellation or refund is used first, in any amount, and credit you earned is used only when it covers the whole fee (Credit Terms §4). The booking screen shows what is charged. If credit covers the whole fee, nothing is charged.
  5. Confirmation. Once payment succeeds, your booking is confirmed and you get a booking code and QR code in the app, or a booking code on the website's booking page. Stripe may email you a receipt.
  6. At the restaurant. Arrive in the booked time window with the number of people booked and show your code. You pay the restaurant directly for your meal, at the discounted price, plus anything not covered by the deal.

6. The discount and the rules for it

  • What it covers. The discount (usually 50%, sometimes a different percentage shown on the time you pick) applies to food from the restaurant's deal menu, or its whole food menu if it hasn't set a deal menu, for the diners in your booking, eaten in, at the booked time.
  • What it doesn't cover. Drinks, tax, tips, service charges, and anything the restaurant page lists as excluded.
  • Order rules. Some restaurants ask each diner to order something alongside the discounted food, for example "1 drink per person" or "1 side or dessert per person". The rules are shown on the restaurant page and the booking screen before you pay. A non-alcoholic drink always counts as a drink. If your table doesn't follow the order rules, the restaurant may decline the discount.
  • One deal per booking. The discount can't be combined with other restaurant offers unless the restaurant says so.
  • Late or different party. If you arrive well after your window or with more people than you booked, the restaurant may decline the discount for the extra people or seat you at full price.
  • Tipping. Tipping is up to you. Many diners tip on the price before the discount.
  • If the restaurant doesn't honour the booking. Tell us within 7 days through the app or at support@eatearlybird.com. If the restaurant refused a valid booking, was closed, wouldn't give the discount when you followed the rules, or charged you more because you booked with Early Bird, we'll refund your booking fee to your card and follow it up with the restaurant. Restaurants that join Early Bird promise to honour their deal as written and not to charge Early Bird guests more.

7. Cancelling

You can cancel a confirmed booking from the booking's page in the app or on the website. A booking made in one shows in the other. What you get back depends on when you cancel:

When you cancel What you get back
Within 60 minutes of booking the whole booking fee, as Early Bird credit
Later, but before the day of the booking half of the booking fee, as Early Bird credit
On the day of the booking, or if you don't show up nothing

The policy is also shown on the booking screen before you pay, and the booking page shows what you'll get back before you confirm a cancellation. Credit from a cancellation expires after 30 days and follows the Credit Terms. Any booking reward is removed when you cancel (Credit Terms §2).

If the restaurant cancels or closes, or we cancel your booking (for example because the restaurant can no longer take it), you get the whole booking fee back to your card.

It is not money and has no cash value. It can't be transferred, sold or exchanged for money, and expires. The Credit Terms set out all the rules and form part of these terms.
