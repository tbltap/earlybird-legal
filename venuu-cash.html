Venuu Cash Terms

Effective 24 November 2026.

These terms explain Venuu cash, a reward balance in your Early Bird account that you earn by sending photos of restaurant receipts. These terms form part of the Early Bird Terms of Service. Early Bird is run by Ngassa Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company.

Right now you can earn Venuu cash, but you can't spend it. No restaurant accepts Venuu cash yet, and we can't promise when or where you'll be able to use it, or that you ever will. We'll update these terms and the app as restaurants join. Everything else in Early Bird, including recommendations, reviews and booking, works as usual.

Venuu cash is separate from Early Bird credit (the Credit Terms). Venuu cash never pays or reduces an Early Bird booking fee, and Early Bird credit is never spent at a restaurant.

1. What Venuu cash is

Venuu cash is a promotional reward that Early Bird funds. It is not money:

  • it has no cash value and is never paid out, withdrawn or refunded as cash;
  • you can't buy it, and you can't transfer, sell, gift or exchange it, including between your own accounts;
  • it isn't a bank account, deposit, gift card or stored-value product, and doesn't earn interest;
  • it can't be spent yet (above), and when it can, it will be only at restaurants that choose to accept it.

Your balance and activity are in the app under Profile → Rewards.

2. How you earn it

Send a photo of a printed receipt in the app, from a restaurant the app shows as earning Venuu cash. You earn 3% of the food on the receipt, before tax, tip and fees, counting up to $150 of food per receipt (so at most $4.50 a receipt). Receipts from visits you booked with an Early Bird deal (for example 50% off food) earn too; the rule on combining Venuu cash with a deal (§4) is only about spending it. The rate and limits are the ones the app shows when you send the receipt, and we may change them (§8).

Limits, to keep it fair:

  • a receipt counts for 48 hours after the date and time printed on it;
  • you can earn from up to 2 visits to the same restaurant a day and 10 receipts a day;
  • receipts from more than 3 restaurants in one day, and receipts with more than $100 of food, are checked by a person before they earn.

3. How receipts are checked

Receipts are read by our AI provider, Anthropic, which reads the restaurant, date and amounts for us (Terms §9). When you take the photo, we also store where it was taken (your device's location at that moment, if you allow location), to check the receipt and prevent fraud (Privacy Policy §2). Clear receipts are checked automatically and paid straight away. A person at Early Bird checks the others, for example edited photos, screenshots and photos of screens, receipts that can't be read clearly, and large totals. We decide receipts we hold, and we may refuse one, giving you the reason.

We refuse:

  • a receipt or photo that has already been paid, whoever sent it;
  • receipts that aren't real or can't be read, that have no date or total, or that are more than 48 hours old;
  • handwritten receipts and notes;
  • receipts from restaurants that don't earn Venuu cash;
  • edited receipts, and receipts from a restaurant you own or work at.

A refused receipt doesn't count against you, but after 3 refused receipts in 30 minutes you'll need to wait 15 minutes before sending another. If a receipt of yours is reversed for fraud (§7), we may check every receipt you send for the next 60 days before it earns.

The Privacy Policy says how receipt photos are handled and how long they are kept.

4. Spending (not available yet)

You can't spend Venuu cash yet. Before you can, we'll update these terms and tell you in the app how spending works and which restaurants accept it.

Not with a deal. When spending opens, Venuu cash can't be combined with an Early Bird deal on the same visit: if you booked with an Early Bird deal (for example 50% off food), you won't be able to pay with Venuu cash on that meal's bill.

5. Who pays for it

Early Bird funds Venuu cash. Restaurants don't pay for the Venuu cash you earn.

6. Expiry and closing your account

While Venuu cash can't be spent anywhere, it doesn't expire. When spending opens, we'll tell you in the app at least 30 days ahead, and Venuu cash will then expire 90 days after the later of the day you earned it and the day spending opened. Expired Venuu cash is removed and can't be brought back.

If you delete your account, or we close it for breaking our terms, your Venuu cash is lost.

7. When we remove it

We may remove or reverse Venuu cash that was paid by mistake, for a receipt that turns out to break §3, or that was earned through fraud: for example, faked or edited receipts, receipts that aren't yours, more than one account, or working with a restaurant. We may also stop the accounts involved from sending receipts, reverse their recent receipts, or suspend or close those accounts. We'll tell you and give you a chance to respond, unless that would help the fraud continue.

8. Changes and ending Venuu cash

We may change how Venuu cash is earned, including the rate and limits, for receipts sent after the change; we'll show the new rate in the app before it applies. Venuu cash you already have isn't reduced by a change. We may pause or end Venuu cash with at least 30 days' notice in the app; if we end it before spending opens, any balance ends with it and has no value.

Questions about Venuu cash: support@eatearlybird.com.
