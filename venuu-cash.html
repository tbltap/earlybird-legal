Venuu Cash Terms

Effective 20 November 2026.

These terms explain Venuu cash, a reward balance in your Early Bird account. You earn it by sending photos of restaurant receipts, and you can spend it only at restaurants that choose to accept it. These terms form part of the Early Bird Terms of Service. Early Bird is run by Ngassa Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company.

Venuu cash is separate from Early Bird credit (the Credit Terms). Venuu cash never pays or reduces an Early Bird booking fee, and Early Bird credit is never spent at a restaurant.

1. What Venuu cash is

Venuu cash is a promotional reward, not money:

  • it has no cash value and is never paid out, withdrawn or refunded as cash;
  • you can't buy it, and you can't transfer, sell, gift or exchange it, including between your own accounts;
  • it expires (§6);
  • it isn't a bank account, deposit, gift card or stored-value product, and doesn't earn interest.

We don't guarantee that you can spend it. It can be used only at restaurants that choose to accept it, and there may be none near you. The app says this where you earn Venuu cash and where your balance shows.

Your balance, activity and expiry dates are in the app under Profile → Rewards.

2. How you earn it

Send a photo of a printed receipt from a restaurant listed on Early Bird, in the app. You earn 3% of the food and drink total, before tax, tip and fees. The rate is the one shown in the app when you send the receipt.

A receipt counts for 7 days after the date printed on it. You earn Venuu cash even if no restaurant near you accepts it yet.

3. How receipts are checked

Asking first. To read a receipt, we send the photo to Anthropic, our AI provider, which reads the restaurant, date and amounts for us. Before your first receipt, the app asks you; nothing is sent until you agree, and if you choose "Not now", nothing is sent and nothing is earned. You can change this at any time in Profile → Privacy. If you withdraw, we stop sending receipts and delete any you sent that haven't been read yet.

Checking. Clear receipts are read automatically, first on your phone and then by our server, and paid straight away. A person at Early Bird checks edited photos, screenshots and photos of screens, receipts that can't be read clearly, and large totals. We decide receipts we hold, and we may refuse one, giving you the reason.

We refuse:

  • a receipt or photo that has already been paid, whoever sent it;
  • receipts that aren't real or can't be read, that have no date or total, or that are more than 7 days old;
  • receipts from restaurants not listed on Early Bird;
  • edited receipts, and receipts from a restaurant you own or work at.

A refused receipt doesn't count against you. To prevent abuse, you can send up to 30 receipts in 24 hours, and a person checks your receipts once you have had 15 accepted in 7 days.

The Privacy Policy says how receipt photos are handled and how long they are kept.

4. How you spend it

You can spend Venuu cash only at restaurants that accept Venuu cash. They are labelled in the app. To spend it:

  1. in the app, choose to pay with Venuu cash at that restaurant and pick an amount, up to your balance;
  2. show the 6-digit code to the staff. It is valid for 15 minutes. Nothing is taken from your balance until the staff confirm the code;
  3. the restaurant takes that amount off your bill.

Each restaurant chooses how much Venuu cash it accepts each month, so it may stop accepting it partway through a month. Venuu cash can't be used to get cash back. If a restaurant confirms a code by mistake, it can reverse it in the same month and the Venuu cash returns to your balance.

5. Why restaurants accept it

Restaurants that accept Venuu cash give you the discount themselves. In return, Early Bird lets them use the Venuu cash they accept to lower what they pay Early Bird for advertising. Early Bird doesn't pay restaurants for it, and doesn't promise that any restaurant will take part.

6. Expiry and closing your account

Venuu cash expires 90 days after you earn it. Expired Venuu cash is removed and can't be brought back. If you delete your account, or we close it for breaking our terms, your Venuu cash is lost.

7. When we remove it

We may remove or reverse Venuu cash that was paid by mistake, for a receipt that turns out to break §3, or that was earned through fraud: for example, faked or edited receipts, receipts that aren't yours, more than one account, or working with a restaurant. We may also suspend or close the accounts involved. We'll tell you and give you a chance to respond, unless that would help the fraud continue.

8. Changes and ending Venuu cash

We may change how Venuu cash is earned, including the rate, for the future at any time; Venuu cash you already have keeps its expiry date. We may end Venuu cash with at least 30 days' notice in the app, during which you can still spend your balance.

9. Contact

Questions about Venuu cash: support@eatearlybird.com.
